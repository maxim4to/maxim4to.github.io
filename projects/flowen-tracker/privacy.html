Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles the information you choose to add, along with the limited product analytics we may use to help improve the experience. We aim to keep the app privacy-conscious, especially when it comes to sensitive health-related information.

01

Data Collection and Storage

We do not store your cycle or health records on our servers. The information you add to the App, including period dates, symptoms, and moods, is saved locally on your device. If you use a backup service such as iCloud or Google Drive, that backup is managed by your provider.

02

No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal health records. We may use privacy-conscious analytics and service providers to understand app usage in aggregate and improve the product, but not to identify you as an individual.

03

Data Retention and Deletion

You can remove local records by deleting them in the app or by deleting the App from your device. Because we do not keep server copies, deleted records may not be recoverable.

04

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

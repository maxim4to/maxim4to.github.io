Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to popular questions about Mars Defence

Is there paid content in the game?

+

No, the game is completely free and contains no in-app purchases.

Can I play without internet?

+

Yes, the game works completely offline. Internet is only needed for showing ads.

Does the game collect my data?

+

Mars Defence has no accounts and keeps your progress on your device. To improve the game, it collects gameplay analytics linked to a device identifier (via Talo). Our advertising partner Google AdMob collects data such as your advertising ID, approximate location and ad interactions to show and measure ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What data does advertising collect?

+

Google AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic data. It uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before ads load, and on any device you can reset or limit your advertising ID in the system settings.

What is App Tracking Transparency?

+

App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is an iOS feature: apps must ask your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. Mars Defence does not ask for this permission, so on iOS it does not get your advertising identifier and does not track you across apps. You can check this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Can I play without ads?

+

Mars Defence only shows optional rewarded ads: an ad plays only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus. If you don't want to see ads, just skip these bonuses. You can also limit ad personalization in your device settings and, in the EEA and the UK, through the consent form.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy in Mars Defence

Mars Defence has no accounts and keeps your game progress on your device. It collects gameplay analytics linked to a device identifier (via Talo) and shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers, approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Read the full Privacy Policy for details, your choices and how to contact us.

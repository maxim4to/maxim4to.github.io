Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Mars Defence, a tower defense game for Android and iOS published by Mi-Crafts, collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Who we are

Mars Defence is provided by Mi-Crafts (Maxim Ivanov, Lisbon, Portugal), referred to as “we” or “us”. We are responsible for the data described below.

  • Contact: maxim.micrafts@gmail.com
  • Website: mi-crafts.com/projects/mars-defence

Summary

  • The game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We collect gameplay analytics linked to a device identifier (not to your name or email address), to improve the game.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device and usage data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, in-game currency, upgrades and settings are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to us. If you delete the game, this data is deleted too.

Gameplay analytics (Talo)

We use Talo, a game analytics service, to understand how the game is played and where players get stuck. Talo processes this data on our behalf. It collects:

  • a device identifier generated by the operating system for this app (it does not identify you by name);
  • gameplay events, such as levels started and completed, tutorial steps, screens opened and upgrades bought;
  • basic technical details: operating system, screen size and window mode.

We use this data only for analytics and to improve the game. We do not sell it and do not use it for advertising.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mars Defence is free and shows ads, including optional rewarded ads, served by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID (device or other identifiers);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance.

This data is used to show ads, measure ad performance, provide analytics and prevent fraud and abuse. Google may use it to personalise ads, depending on your consent and device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are loaded, the game shows a consent form provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can agree or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • iOS: ads can use your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can stop all data collection by the game by uninstalling it.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the services described above: Google (AdMob) for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, and Talo for analytics. We do not sell your personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics data in Talo is kept only as long as needed to analyse and improve the game. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s own retention policies.

Because the game has no accounts, analytics data is linked only to a device identifier. To ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your device, email maxim.micrafts@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at maxim.micrafts@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Mars Defence is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or the services it uses change. The current version is always published on this page with its “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Mars Defence: maxim.micrafts@gmail.com.

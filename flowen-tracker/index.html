Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our mobile application (the "App") handles your information. Your privacy is our top priority, especially regarding sensitive health data.

01

Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, store, or process your personal health data on any external servers. All information you enter into the App (including period dates, symptoms, and moods) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data, and it never leaves your phone unless you manually perform a backup via your OS provider (e.g., iCloud/Google Drive).

02

No Third-Party Sharing

Since we do not collect your data, we do not share, sell, or trade your personal information with any third parties, advertising networks, or analytics companies.

03

Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. All records are deleted immediately if you delete the App from your device. Because we do not store your data on servers, we cannot recover it for you if it is lost or deleted.

04

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children because, by design, we do not collect information from any users.
